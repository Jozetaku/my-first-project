Before you book · precautions and repeat visits

These are complementary wellness and cosmetic sessions. If you have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device, skip electrical or magnetic device sessions. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a serious heart condition, uncontrolled high blood pressure, an acute illness or an infection, seek medical advice before booking. Tell Joe about sensitivities before choosing skincare. Stop the session if a setting feels painful or uncomfortable.

Thinking of booking several visits? Ask Joe to check the instructions for your specific device and attachment before setting a schedule.
